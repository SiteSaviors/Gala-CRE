# Gala CRE company story register

Last researched: September 22, 2026

## Purpose

This register separates public evidence from proposed positioning for the `/company` rebuild. It is authoritative for company-history, affiliation, market-footprint, and company-imagery decisions. It does not replace `PROPERTY-DATA-REGISTER.md`, which remains authoritative for property and transaction facts.

Publication labels:

- **Publishable** — supported by current client direction and a public source or the approved site record.
- **Publishable with qualification** — usable only with the limits stated here.
- **Client confirmation required** — plausible or publicly represented elsewhere, but not established strongly enough for Gala CRE copy.
- **Do not publish** — unsupported, conflicting, confidential, or outside the relevant entity.

- **Leadership:** Gaurang Gala is CEO and Broker-in-Charge; North Carolina license 283149.
- **Office:** 105 Kilmayne Drive, Suite C, Cary, North Carolina 27511.

- **Current audience:** property owners, investors, landlords, tenants, developers, entrepreneurs, and business operators.
- **Current service pillars:** Brokerage, Investment Sales, Development, Capital Markets, and Property Management Partnership, subject to the direct/coordination/partner-delivered distinctions already encoded in the site.

### Publishable with qualification

- Gaurang brings experience from commercial/SBA lending, brokerage, real estate investment, land, zoning, entitlements, and development partnerships. This can support a **developer-informed brokerage perspective**.
- Gala CRE can describe its advice as considering the consequences beyond the immediate transaction: market position, entitlement and site constraints, capital, diligence, and execution. This is a positioning statement, not a representation that Gala CRE directly performs every discipline.
- Public sources establish Gaurang's leadership or principal roles across Gala CRE, Gala Investments, and Radius. Copy may say that Gala CRE's perspective is informed by broader development and investment experience. It must not state that the companies are one legal platform.

## “The Developer's Brokerage” — supported meaning

The strongest evidence-backed interpretation is:

> Brokerage advice informed by how developers and investors evaluate land, entitlements, capital, risk, marketability, and execution—not only by the transaction itself.

This direction is supported by Gaurang's public brokerage profile, the Gala Investments development focus, the Radius land-and-entitlement platform, and his podcast discussion of value creation before construction.

Safe themes for future Company copy:

- See the whole opportunity, not only the listing or lease.
- Bring development, diligence, capital, and transaction considerations into the conversation early.
- Help clients understand the tradeoffs that affect execution after a brokerage decision.
- Connect the appropriate internal capability or outside professional without implying that Gala directly provides legal, tax, engineering, environmental, construction, or securities advice.

## Why Gala CRE was created

**Client confirmation required.** No public source located in this checkpoint gives a direct founding statement for Gala CRE or Gala Real Estate Advisors LLC.

The research supports—but does not independently prove—the following working hypothesis:

> Gala CRE was created to bring the practical perspective gained through lending, investment, land, entitlement, and development work into a more connected commercial brokerage experience.

Before this becomes public copy, the client should confirm or correct that sentence and identify the person speaking for the company.

## Phase 1 publication decision

The Company rebuild may proceed using the developer-informed brokerage thesis, the approved service model, the shared Team records, the current property/transaction catalog, and News links.

The following must remain omitted or qualified until the client responds:

1. Gala CRE's founding year and formal origin story.
2. “Two decades” or any combined experience metric.
3. Legal ownership or corporate-family claims among Gala CRE, Gala Investments, Radius, Legacy Carolina Development, and Gala Realty Group.
4. A service territory broader than Raleigh-Durham and the Research Triangle.
5. Affiliated-company projects, performance metrics, logos, or photography.
